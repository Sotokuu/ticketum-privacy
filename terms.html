Ticketum Backup · Last updated November 5, 2026

Ticketum Backup is an administrator-operated tool for backing up Ticketum. It is intended for the account owner and authorized administrators, not as a public backup service.

Authorized use

Only connect Google accounts and back up information that you are authorized to administer. Keep access credentials and encryption keys secure. The Privacy Policy explains the intended processing of information.

Prepared functionality

The automation is prepared but the Google Drive connection and complete cloud backup process have not yet been validated. Viewing this website does not activate the tool. Availability, successful execution and recoverability must be checked by the administrator; these pages make no uptime or recovery guarantee.

Recovery responsibility

Maintain a separate protected copy of the encryption key and test recovery in an isolated environment. Revoking Google access does not erase existing backups. Changes to backup automation, retention or recovery destinations require an administrative decision.
