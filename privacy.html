Privacy Policy

Ticketum Backup · Last updated November 5, 2026

This policy explains how Ticketum Backup is designed to access, use, store and share information when its owner enables administrative backups. It covers the backup tool and this informational website. Contact: jond50736@gmail.com.

The automation is prepared but its Google Drive connection and complete cloud backup process have not yet been validated. The descriptions below explain its intended configured operation, not a claim that automatic backups are already active.

Purpose and information processed

The tool is intended for administrative backup and recovery of Ticketum. Database backups may contain personal information held in Ticketum, including account records, customer names and contact details, booking or seat information, attendance, and internal notes. These records originate from Ticketum, not from unrelated files in Google Drive.

When authorized, the tool processes OAuth credentials needed to maintain the connection and metadata about its managed Google Drive folders and files, such as identifiers, names, sizes and backup dates. It uploads encrypted backup files, reads them back to verify integrity, and manages retention. It does not request Gmail, Google Contacts, or general access to every file in the Google account.

Google permissions and use

The requested scope is https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. This allows access to files created by the application or explicitly made available to it. The prepared integration uses this permission to create a private backup folder, upload and verify backup files, list its managed recovery points, and move expired managed backups to the trash. Files outside that managed set are not intended to be modified.

Information obtained through Google APIs is used only to provide and maintain the backup functionality described here. It is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train general-purpose artificial intelligence models. Use and any transfer of Google API information are limited to these disclosed purposes and the applicable Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Storage and security

The prepared workflow encrypts database dumps and recovery metadata using AES-256-GCM before sending them to Google Drive. It checks archive integrity locally and verifies uploaded bytes using SHA-256. The encryption key is kept separately from the Drive backup files; a recovery copy of the key must be retained securely by the owner. Limited technical metadata, such as backup dates and checksums, may remain unencrypted.

OAuth credentials and backup credentials are intended to be stored as protected automation secrets, not in the public repository or this website. Backup work takes place in a temporary automation environment. Credentials are removed when the process finishes; temporary archives are cleaned after a verified upload or when the temporary environment is destroyed. Encryption and checks cannot guarantee that data loss or unauthorized access will never occur.

Service providers and access

The prepared workflow uses GitHub Actions to process the backup and Google Drive to store encrypted files. These providers process information necessary to deliver their services under their own terms and policies. Their services may process information in different countries. Access to decrypted backup contents is intended to be restricted to authorized administrators with the required key and a legitimate backup or recovery purpose.

Retention and deletion

When enabled and successful, rotation is configured to retain a combined set of 7 daily, 4 weekly and 3 monthly recovery points. A single backup can represent more than one interval. Rotation follows successful creation and verification of a new backup; failed backup attempts do not trigger removal of older recovery points.

Expired copies managed by this installation are moved to the Google Drive trash. Trash retention and final removal follow Google's rules and the account owner's actions. Incomplete uploads may remain for administrative review. Revoking OAuth permission does not automatically delete existing backups; the owner can remove those files directly in Drive. A restored or separately retained copy may have its own retention period.

Your choices

The Google account owner can review and revoke the tool's access through Google Account connections. Revocation prevents future authorized operations once existing credentials are no longer accepted. To ask about access, correction or deletion of personal information held in a Ticketum backup, contact jond50736@gmail.com. Requests must be assessed in the context of the relevant Ticketum records and recovery copies; no fixed response time is promised by this policy.

This public website

This static website contains no application login, analytics, third-party scripts or tracking cookies added by Ticketum Backup. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; the hosting provider may process ordinary request information such as IP addresses and browser information under its privacy statement. Opening these pages does not authorize Google Drive access or activate backups.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated when the backup tool's practices change. The date above identifies the latest version. Questions about this policy can be sent to jond50736@gmail.com.
